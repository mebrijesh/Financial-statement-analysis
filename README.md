### Project Overview

The project titled **“Financial Statement Analysis of Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) Limited”** was undertaken to understand and evaluate the financial performance and financial position of one of India’s leading IT services companies. The main objective of the project was to analyze the company’s financial statements and understand how financial data can be used to evaluate business performance, profitability, liquidity, solvency, and operational efficiency.

Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) is a global information technology services, consulting, and business solutions company and is a part of the Tata Group. For this project, financial information of TCS was collected from reliable sources, including the company’s official website, annual reports, investor presentations, Screener, and Moneycontrol. The collected information mainly included the Profit and Loss Statement, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow Statement for selected financial years.

The project began with the collection and organization of raw financial data. The data was entered and arranged systematically in **Microsoft Excel** to make the analysis easier and more accurate. Different financial figures such as revenue, expenses, operating profit, net profit, assets, liabilities, shareholders’ equity, cash flows, and other important financial components were studied. Historical financial data was compared to identify changes and trends in the company’s performance over the selected period.

A major part of the project involved **ratio analysis**. Various financial ratios were calculated to understand different aspects of TCS’s financial performance. Profitability ratios were used to evaluate the company’s ability to generate profits from its operations and investments. Liquidity ratios helped assess the company’s ability to meet its short-term financial obligations. Solvency-related measures were considered to understand the company’s financial stability and debt position. Efficiency ratios were also analyzed to understand how effectively the company utilizes its available resources.

Along with ratio analysis, **year-on-year comparison and trend analysis** were performed to identify the growth or decline in important financial indicators. The analysis helped in understanding changes in revenue, profitability, assets, liabilities, and cash flows. Graphs and charts were prepared in Excel to present the findings in a simple and understandable manner. This visual representation made it easier to identify financial trends and compare the company’s performance across different years.

The project also provided practical exposure to financial analysis and data interpretation. It helped in developing skills in **Microsoft Excel, financial statement analysis, data organization, ratio calculation, financial interpretation, and presentation**. Microsoft Word was used for preparing and documenting the project report, while Canva was used to improve the visual presentation of project-related content. AI-based automation tools were also used as supporting tools for organizing information, improving presentation, and assisting during the project preparation process.

Overall, the project provided a practical understanding of how financial statements can be analyzed to evaluate the financial health and performance of a company. The study of TCS helped connect theoretical concepts of financial management with real-world corporate financial data and improved the ability to interpret financial information for academic and professional purposes.
